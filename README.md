# Bayesian Model-Based and Distance-Based Clustering: A Comparison

In this project, two algorithms for clustering discrete data in a Bayesian framework were compared. In particular, the aim was to shed light on the main differences between Model-Based clustering and Distance-Based clustering in an uncommon setting. To conduct this comparison, two main clustering techniques were employed: mixture models of multinomials, through the Latent Class Model, and the generalized Bayes product partition model, a distance-based approach implemented within a generalized Bayesian framework. The comparison was carried out through a simulation study considering three different scenarios. Finally, to assess the validity of the proposed methods, an application was developed using a survey conducted by the Department of Statistics and Actuarial Science at Stellenbosch University on the effects of alcohol on academic performance.
